# Audit and improvements, 2026-11-13

Baseline: `flutter analyze` 6 errors + 11 infos; `flutter test` 60 passing, 2
files failing to load (`era_highlight_test.dart` called a `focusRegions` that
did not exist, which also broke `app_test.dart` in the same run). The user's
uncommitted work (28 modified files, 8 new) was left in place and snapshotted
before any change.

Result: `flutter analyze` clean; `flutter test` 74/74; `tools/test_nav.py`
0 failures; `flutter build apk --release` builds (58.8 MB).

## What the rendering actually is

No 3D engine. Every plan is drawn by `CustomPainter`s in `museum_map_canvas.dart`:
the official SVG plan as a bitmap, rooms and walls extruded into prisms and
projected through `IsoCamera` (a tilt plus gentle perspective, `Matrix4`), and
painted back to front. Monuments are either upright silhouettes or real
triangle meshes (`gem_figures.dart`, generated by `tools/build_figures.py`)
drawn with `Canvas.drawVertices`. So: 2.5D extrusion, with true 3D meshes for
the figures. Nothing loads glTF at runtime.

## Verified, and how

* Off-screen renders of the real app in `flutter test` at 393×851 pt with real
  fonts (Liberation for Arial/Georgia, Noto for Arabic and CJK): routes, step
  stepping, a trip in English and Arabic, the Prayer Room, and every language.
* Directions plotted over the official plan to check each turn's side.
* The physical Android phone was used only for one baseline screenshot: the
  owner was using it, so device testing stopped there.

## Not done, and why

* **On-device walkthrough** of the new guidance (the phone was in use). Run
  `flutter run` and walk a route and a Rostau trip.
* **Native-speaker review** of the flagged renderings in `docs/i18n-glossary.md`.
  The six translated stories other than attraction 5 were spot-checked, not
  re-translated.
* **Prayer Room door and qibla**: no source shows them; if the museum confirms
  them, add them in `tools/blender/prayer_room.py` and rerun the build.
* **Map proportions** were not re-traced: the plans come from the official PDF
  vectors, and `test_nav.py` already holds the walls, floor and graph to them.
* The Python tools need numpy, scipy, scikit-image, OpenCV, shapely,
  svgpathtools and `rsvg-convert`, none installed system-wide here; they were
  run from a throwaway venv with a cairosvg stand-in for `rsvg-convert`.
